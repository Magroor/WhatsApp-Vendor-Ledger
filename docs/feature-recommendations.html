KhataLink product recommendations

High-impact features to build next

Ranked from what the app does today and what testing showed: bills arrive as messy text, photos and screenshots; vendors are often saved under different names; and the owner's time is the scarce resource. Impact is measured by hours saved, money recovered or mistakes avoided.

Build first (biggest payoff, smallest effort)

Rank 1

One-tap review queue

Problem: unclear bills pile up as drafts and each needs several taps.

How: a swipe-style queue: bill photo on top, detected amount and vendor below, big Bill / Paid buttons, "Confirm all clean ones" for high-confidence items. Builds on the new Bill / Paid chooser.

Impact: very highEffort: smallUses existing chooser
Rank 2

Vendor aliases and bill-phone matching

Problem: the handwritten Urdu bill stayed "Unassigned" because the printed name did not match the saved vendor.

How: each vendor gets extra names (Urdu, English, nickname) and extra phone numbers. After the first manual assignment, the app remembers the bill's name or phone and matches it next time. Fuzzy match on Urdu and Roman Urdu spellings.

Impact: very highEffort: smallFixes "Unassigned" bills
Rank 3

Send statement to vendor on WhatsApp

Problem: owners settle accounts by phone call and memory.

How: a button on each vendor: generates a clean PDF or text statement (bills, payments, balance) and opens WhatsApp with it ready to send (share sheet on iPhone). Disputes end faster because both sides see the same numbers.

Impact: highEffort: smallReports exist
Rank 4

"Who to pay this week" cash-flow planner

Problem: aging reports show numbers, but not a decision.

How: a Home card listing overdue and due-soon bills by vendor, with a total needed this week and a "Mark paid" shortcut that records the payment. Sorted by due date and by vendor terms.

Impact: highEffort: smallAging data exists
Rank 5

Push notifications (installed iPhone app)

Problem: nothing tells the owner a bill arrived or a payment is due.

How: web push (iOS 16.4+, home-screen app only) for: new bill needs review, vendor payment due tomorrow, weekly summary. Opt-in per type.

Impact: highEffort: mediumNeeds push keys + service worker work
Rank 6

Partial payments matched to bills

Problem: a payment of Rs 5,000 against a Rs 13,500 bill is just a number; open balance per bill is unclear.

How: allocate each payment to specific bills (auto oldest-first, editable), show "Rs 8,500 still open on Bill 67", mark bills settled. The aging engine already does oldest-first matching; this exposes and saves it.

Impact: highEffort: medium
Rank 7

Item price history and alerts

Problem: owners rarely notice a vendor quietly raising rates.

How: track unit price per item per vendor from parsed bills. Show a trend line and alert: "Cement from Ahmed Traders is up 8% since last month". Compare the same item across vendors.

Impact: highEffort: mediumOrder details already captured
Rank 8

Duplicate and mismatch detection

Problem: the same bill arrives as a photo, a forwarded copy and a typed message; typos change totals.

How: flag likely duplicates (same vendor, amount and date window) and check that item lines add up to the total. Show a yellow warning before confirming.

Impact: highEffort: small
Rank 9

Customers (receivables) as well as vendors

Problem: most small shops also sell on credit and track who owes them.

How: a second ledger type for customers using the same engine, with "send reminder" via WhatsApp. This roughly doubles who can use the app.

Impact: very highEffort: largeNew market segment
Rank 10

Urdu interface and Urdu numerals

Problem: many shop owners and helpers are more comfortable in Urdu.

How: language toggle (English / اردو) with right-to-left layout, Urdu digits optional, and Urdu month names in reports.

Impact: highEffort: medium
Rank 11

Backup, export everything, and offline capture queue

Problem: accounts are business-critical; poor mobile signal loses photos.

How: one-tap full export (CSV and photos zip) and an automatic weekly backup file. Queue scanned bills on the phone when offline and upload them when the connection returns.

Impact: highEffort: medium
Rank 12

Voice notes to entries

Problem: vendors often send voice notes ("5 hazar bhej diye"), which are ignored today.

How: transcribe voice notes with a free speech model, run them through the existing Roman Urdu parser, and send the result to the review queue. Works for notes shared from WhatsApp and recorded in the app.

Impact: highEffort: medium

Reliability work that supports every feature

  • Photo reading under load: Gemini's free tier is sometimes busy. Add a visible Retry on drafts and a background retry queue.
  • Speed: keep the server in the same region as the database and show instant loading screens (done); cache vendor lists.
  • Confidence you can see: show "read from photo, 72% sure" and the original bill beside every draft entry.
  • Security: per-user logins and read-only accountant access before sharing the app with staff.
Based on the current app (WhatsApp text and photo capture, chat import, Roman Urdu parsing, reports, review inbox) and the test results from real bills and screenshots.
